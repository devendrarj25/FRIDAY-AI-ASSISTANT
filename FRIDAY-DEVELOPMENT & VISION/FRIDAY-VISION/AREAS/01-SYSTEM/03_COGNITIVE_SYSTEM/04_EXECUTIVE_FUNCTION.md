# Executive Function and Human-Like Behavior Boundary

The system should behave coherently like a capable human executive assistant without pretending to be conscious.

Core loops:
- **Attention:** what matters now, what is urgent, what can wait.
- **Working set:** current turn/task context with bounded context budgets.
- **Goals:** desired outcomes and measurable success conditions.
- **Commitments:** promises FRIDAY made, deadlines, pending approvals and follow-ups.
- **Reflection:** compare plan vs outcome and identify causes.
- **Affect:** use stable behavioral state to tune communication, urgency and persistence—not to invent emotions as facts.
- **Beliefs:** store confidence-weighted claims with source/time and contradictions.
- **Social model:** people, roles, preferences, interaction history and communication norms.
- **Self-model:** current capabilities, health, limits, active tasks and known uncertainties.

Human-like does not mean unrestricted. The executive layer must remain subordinate to policy and owner authority.
