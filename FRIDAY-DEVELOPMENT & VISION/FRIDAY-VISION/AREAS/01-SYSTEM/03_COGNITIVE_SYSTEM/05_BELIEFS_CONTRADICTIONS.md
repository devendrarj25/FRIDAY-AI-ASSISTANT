# Beliefs, Uncertainty and Contradictions

Every important world claim should carry:
- subject/entity;
- predicate/fact;
- source and provenance;
- observed/valid time;
- confidence;
- freshness/expiry policy;
- scope;
- contradiction set;
- resolution status.

When two sources conflict, do not silently overwrite. Keep both claims, score source authority and freshness, attempt reconciliation, and mark unresolved conflicts for the executive layer. A lower-confidence assumption must never silently outrank a newer authoritative observation.

This enables FRIDAY to say: “I have conflicting information; source A says X, source B says Y; I will verify before acting.”
